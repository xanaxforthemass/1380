Part A questions:
1. What are the base case and recursive case for your factorial?
    The base case  is n <= 1. The recursive case is n * factorial(n - 1).
2. What goes wrong if you omit the base case?
    Infinite recursion can lead to stack overflow.
3. Why does naive fib(n) repeat work? (One or two sentences is enough.)
    Naive fib(n) repeats work because it does not remember previous values that may have already been calculated, such as fib(2)
    being calculated once as the n-2 in fib(4), and once as the n-1 in fib(3).
